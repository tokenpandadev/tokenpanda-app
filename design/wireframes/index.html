tokenpanda wireframes

tokenpanda as it is built (main at 4ca4160, release 0.1.7), every screen and state the user flows touch, on a 1280 by 800 desktop with the menu bar and the popover. 80 screens, 14 flows, 83 steps tested against the real app: 51 work, 20 with friction, 12 broken. Controls that lead somewhere are links; the popover keys work too (Esc, Tab, Shift Tab, m, p, s). Other outcomes and other starts are dashed targets: press h on any screen to show them and outline every link. Coral notes name what is wrong on a screen. The findings, the gap list and the fixes are in flow-test.md; the flows as text and diagrams in flows/.
