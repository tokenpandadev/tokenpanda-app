tokenpanda wireframes

tokenpanda as it is built (main at 274b15c, 0.1.15), every screen and state the user flows touch, on a 1280 by 800 desktop with the menu bar and the popover. 90 screens, 14 flows, 105 steps tested against the real app: 86 work, 6 with friction, 0 broken. Controls that lead somewhere are links; the popover keys work too (Esc, Tab, Shift Tab, m, p, s). Other outcomes and other starts are dashed targets: press h on any screen to show them and outline every link. Coral notes name friction on a screen, grey notes say how it works and where in the code. flow-test.md keeps the first flow test (0.1.7) with its gap list, under the steps as of 0.1.14; the flows as text and diagrams in flows/.
