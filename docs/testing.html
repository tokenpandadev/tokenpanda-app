1 Before you start

  • Run the app bundle (scripts/build-app.sh, then open build/tokenpanda.app), not swift run: notifications and the version line need the bundle.
  • Have one Claude Code session open, so the Claude tab shows windows and a session row.
  • Turn VoiceOver on and off with Command F5. VO below means Control Option, the VoiceOver keys.
  • VoiceOver's exact words depend on its verbosity setting. Below is what each element should carry: its name, its state and its kind. The order of the words may differ.

2 Keyboard only, VoiceOver off

  1. Click the panda in the menu bar. The popover opens. No ring is drawn yet.
  2. Press Down. A thin accent ring appears around "Claude" in the tab strip.
  3. Press Right twice. The ring moves to "Mac", then to "Settings". Press Right again: it stays on "Settings".
  4. Press Space. The Settings tab opens; the ring stays on "Settings".
  5. Press Down. The ring is on "[ ] Open at login" (box and label together). Keep pressing Down: each setting, then "Install the status line entry" or "Remove the status line entry", then "Quit". Up goes back the same way.
  6. On "[x] List price equivalent", press Space: the box empties. Press Return: it is ticked again. Open the Claude tab with Tab: the price chip came back.
  7. Press Tab. The Claude tab opens and the ring sits on "Claude". Shift Tab goes back to Settings, the ring on "Settings".
  8. On the Claude tab, Down from the tabs first puts the ring around the Weekly section, then the 5h section, and at the end each week over week row; with VoiceOver on, each is said whole ("Weekly, all models. 61% used. ..."). On the Mac tab Down stops on CPU, GPU, memory and battery the same way.
  9. On the Claude tab press Down until the ring is on a session's figure ("405.6k of 1M 41%"): its tooltip appears above it, over two lines when it is long, nothing cut. Down again reaches "Models", "Projects", "Sessions" (Left and Right along that line, Space selects), then the model rows, each with its tooltip.
  10. Click anywhere in the popover: the ring goes. Press Down: it comes back where it was.
  11. Esc closes the popover. Command Q quits.

3 VoiceOver

  1. With VoiceOver on, VO M M (the menu extras) and VO Right to the panda. Heard: "tokenpanda, Weekly 61% used, over pace, resets Sunday 18:00", and "button". The weekday is said in full.
  2. VO Space opens the popover. If VoiceOver stays on the menu bar, press VO Shift Down to go into the popover, or VO F5 (the item chooser) and pick the panda. VO Right through it. The order is the reading order of the panel, top to bottom.
  3. Right after the header line, VoiceOver goes to the tabs. The thin rule under the header is not read at all; if you hear "upper one eighth block" or similar, that is a bug.
  4. The tab strip. Heard: "Tabs, tab group", then for each tab its name, "selected" on the open one, and "tab" (for example "Claude, selected, tab, 1 of 3"). With the Mac warning on, the middle tab says "Mac, warning".
  5. VO Space on "Settings": the Settings tab opens and the tab now says selected.
  6. A setting. Heard: "List price equivalent, checked, checkbox". After a pause, its help: "tokens at list prices, not the plan price". VO Right goes straight to the next setting: the dim line is said once, as help, not again as text. VO Space: "unchecked". VO Space again: "checked". The panel changes with it.
  7. "Keep the Mac awake while Claude works" has the help "a closed lid on battery still sleeps".
  8. "Install the status line entry" or "Remove the status line entry": "button". Its dim lines under it are read as text after it, each wrapped note once and whole (for example "one entry in each of ~/.claude, ~/.claude-work" when two settings folders are found). Do not press it on your own Mac unless you mean to; use a test account, or Remove then Install again.
  9. "Quit, command Q, button". Do not press it during the test.
  10. The Claude tab. Each window section is one element, one sentence, no glyph names: "Weekly, all models. 61% used. Resets Sunday 18:00, in 3 days 3 hours. Over pace, limit Sunday 01:20 at this rate. 0.5% an hour left." A session row: "workspace, Fable 5.1, context 405.6 thousand of 1 million, 41% used", with its tooltip as help. "Models", "Projects", "Sessions": "Token sections, tab group", each a tab with its state. A model row: "Fable 5.1, 1.02 billion tokens, 49% of 7 days", the figure once. Week over week: "Week of 28 September, 100%, hit the limit Thursday 16:10".
  11. The Mac tab. Heard: "CPU 9%, user 5%, system 4%, 10 cores", "GPU ...", then "Memory", then one image: "Memory 25.5 of 36 gigabytes, 71%, free 10.5 gigabytes, swap 0.9 gigabytes, pressure normal". The two hundred grid cells and the legend glyphs are not read one by one. Then "Battery 75%, charging, ..." and the process rows, "Safari, 6.1% CPU, 6.2 GB memory".
  12. Pointer: with VoiceOver on, move the mouse over the popover (in VoiceOver Utility, Navigation, let the VoiceOver cursor follow the mouse pointer), or use the trackpad commander. Over a tab, a checkbox or a row, VoiceOver says that element, not the whole panel.
  13. The menu bar item while stale: quit Claude Code, wait over an hour, VO on the panda. Heard: "Weekly 61% used, ..., as of 13:05" with the time of the last figures.
  14. With the keyboard focus on (press Down once, VoiceOver still on): VoiceOver follows the ring and says the focused element as the ring moves.
  15. Install error: with a test account whose ~/.claude/settings.json is not valid JSON, on the Claude tab VO Space on "Install the status line entry". Heard after it, as text: "Could not install the entry: cannot read settings.json: settings.json is not valid JSON", over two or three lines, nothing cut.

Note anything that is silent, said twice, said out of order, or has no state. Each is a bug.

4 Text size and contrast

  • The popover is SF Mono 12 and does not follow System Settings, Accessibility, Display, Text size: AppKit gives an app no size to read (see the style guide, section 9). To enlarge it, use Displays, a Larger Text resolution, or Zoom. Check the popover there: same layout, larger.
  • Put a white wallpaper behind the menu bar and open the popover. The key hints at the foot must read clearly; the reset and pace lines (dim) should too. Their ratios on the app's own render are in the style guide, section 9.

5 Panel height and the hidden icon

The panel on a short screen

  1. In System Settings, Displays, pick the "Larger Text" end of the resolutions, or use a screen under 800 points tall. Open the popover on the Mac tab, then on Settings with Keep the Mac awake ticked; those are the tallest tabs.
  2. The panel ends a little above the bottom of the screen, with room for the arrow under the menu bar; it never runs off the screen. It is never shorter than 200 points.
  3. Scroll with two fingers or the mouse wheel: the lines move inside the glass, header and tab strip included, and a thin scroller shows at the right while you scroll and hides again. Quit and the key hints at the foot can be scrolled to.
  4. Press Down until the ring passes the bottom edge: the panel scrolls so the focused control stays in view with a line of room around it. Up scrolls back the same way.
  5. Scroll to an end and push on: the lines stop, no rubber band. The scroller lies over the lines and takes no width, also with "Show scroll bars: Always" in System Settings, Appearance.
  6. Click another tab, or close and open the popover: it starts at the top. Each tab is only as tall as its lines, up to the same limit. On a full size screen nothing scrolls and no scroller shows.
  7. Scroll so a figure sits on the first visible line and focus or hover it: its tooltip opens below the line, inside the panel.
  8. With VoiceOver on, VO Right through every line to the foot: each line, text as well as controls, scrolls into view as VoiceOver reaches it, with a line of room, and its outline stays inside the panel. This needs "Keyboard focus follows the VoiceOver cursor" on in VoiceOver Utility, Navigation, as it is by default; note whether a text line stays outside the visible part (spec section 20.5.3). 0.1.14 scrolled for controls only, on every macOS version.

The hidden icon window

It runs once, a few seconds after the first launch of the app bundle, and marks itself done in the preferences. To see it again, quit the app and run defaults delete dev.tokenpanda.app hiddenItemHintShown.

  1. On macOS 26 and later: quit the app, delete the key as above, and in System Settings, Menu Bar, switch tokenpanda off. Open the app.
  2. About five seconds later one window: "tokenpanda is running", with "Open Menu Bar Settings" and "OK". On a Mac with a notch: "Its icon is not visible in the menu bar. It may be switched off in System Settings, Menu Bar, hidden behind the notch when the menu bar is full, or kept out of view by an app that hides menu bar icons, such as Bartender or Ice." On a Mac without one: "Its icon is not visible in the menu bar. It may be switched off in System Settings, Menu Bar, left out when the menu bar is full, or kept out of view by an app that hides menu bar icons, such as Bartender or Ice."
  3. "Open Menu Bar Settings" opens System Settings at Menu Bar. Switch tokenpanda on: the panda appears.
  4. Before macOS 26, on a Mac with a notch: fill the menu bar with other apps until the panda is behind the notch, delete the key, open the app. The window says "Its icon is not visible in the menu bar, most likely because the menu bar is full and the icon is hidden behind the notch. Quitting another menu bar app makes room for it. An app that hides menu bar icons, such as Bartender or Ice, can also keep it out of view.", with "OK" only and no pane named.
  5. Before macOS 26, on a Mac without a notch (a Mac mini, an iMac, a Mac Studio, a MacBook without one), the same way: the window says "Its icon is not visible in the menu bar, most likely because the menu bar is full. Quitting another menu bar app makes room for it. An app that hides menu bar icons, such as Bartender or Ice, can also keep it out of view." It names no notch.
  6. With a menu bar manager (Bartender, Ice) set to hide tokenpanda, delete the key and open the app: the window comes, on any version, and names that kind of app. Note if it does not come: the check was read from the code only.
  7. Quit and open the app again: no window. It shows once.
  8. Lock the screen: delete the key, open the app and press Control Command Q within five seconds. Unlock after a minute. The window comes only after the unlock, within about 30 seconds, never on the lock screen. The same holds for a sleeping display and for another user switched in with Fast User Switching.
  9. With the item visible, delete the key and open the app: no window. log show --info --last 5m --predicate 'subsystem == "dev.tokenpanda.app"' has "status item visible, no menu bar hint needed".

Notifications turned off and on

  1. With the app running and Nudge ticked, turn tokenpanda's notifications off in System Settings, Notifications. Open the panel on Settings: "Notifications are off in System Settings" stands under the Nudge row, in the accent.
  2. Close the panel, turn the notifications on again, open the panel: the note is gone, with no restart.
  3. Turn them off again and open the panel: the note is back.
  4. A nudge held back (spec 20.6.2): with notifications off and the weekly at 80% or more, and no "Weekly at" banner yet this week, wait a minute, then turn notifications on in System Settings without opening the panel. Within about a minute, while a session feeds, one "Weekly at N%" banner comes with the figure of that moment; it does not come again in that window, also not after a quit and a new launch.
  5. The same with the 5h window: turned on inside its last 15 minutes, "5h window ends in 15 min" comes once; turned on after its reset, nothing comes for it.

6 What the tests cover

swift test checks, without VoiceOver: the role, state, label and help of every control; that a press runs the same action as a click; the order the arrows move the focus in; that the focus and the VoiceOver elements survive a render; that no line of any sample screen runs past the panel; that a long tooltip breaks over lines; that the key hints keep 4.5 to 1 and the free memory cells 3 to 1 over a white desktop; that the panel height is capped at the screen's visible height less 60 points and never under 200; that the hidden icon window offers the Menu Bar switch only from macOS 26, names the notch only on a Mac with one and names a menu bar manager; that the notification permission maps from what System Settings says, and that a stale read, one sent before a newer read or before the answer to the prompt, never writes over a newer one (with reads it plays out by hand, not the notification centre itself); and that a line VoiceOver focuses scrolls into view in a capped panel. What it cannot check is what VoiceOver says out loud and whether it sets that focus, how the panel scrolls on a real screen, what System Settings answers, and whether the hidden icon window comes. That is this page.
